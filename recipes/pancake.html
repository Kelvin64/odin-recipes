Pancake

Description

Pancakes are a type of flat, round cake that is made from a batter of flour, sugar, eggs, and milk. They are cooked on a hot surface, such as a griddle or frying pan, and are often served with syrup, butter, or other toppings.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Vanilla

Steps

  1. Mix the ingredients
  2. Cook on a hot surface
  3. Serve with syrup, butter, or other toppings
