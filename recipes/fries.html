Fries

Description

Fries are a type of potato that is fried in a pot of oil. They are often served with a sauce of ketchup, mustard, or other toppings.

Ingredients

  • Potato
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Cut the potato
  2. Fry the potato
  3. Serve with sauce, mustard, or other toppings
