Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a type of pasta that is made from a dough of flour, eggs, and water. It is cooked in a pot of boiling water and is often served with a sauce of tomato, cheese, and meat.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Water
  • Tomato
  • Cheese
  • Meat

Steps

  1. Mix the ingredients
  2. Cook on a hot surface
  3. Serve with sauce, cheese, and meat
